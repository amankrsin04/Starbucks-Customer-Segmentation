Recommendation Methodology

Our recommendation pipeline employs a two-tier evaluation engine:

1. K-Means Behavioral Classification: Standardizes customer demographics and purchasing frequency to assign them to one of 5 empirical segments.
2. Supervised Conversion Forecast: A trained Random Forest Classifier predicts completion probability for every available offer, preventing feature leakage and weighting affordability.
✓ Validated with Stratified 80/20 Test Separation (ROC-AUC: 0.9027, Accuracy: 83.68%).
